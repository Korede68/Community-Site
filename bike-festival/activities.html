Activities

A full day, from morning group rides to evening wind-down. Drop in for one activity or stick around for all of them.

10:00 AM

Beginner Group Ride — Waterfront Loop

A relaxed 8 km ride for first-timers and families, led by volunteer ride captains. All skill levels welcome.

10:30 AM

Kids' Bike Rodeo

A short obstacle course teaching young riders signaling, braking, and basic road awareness. Helmets required (spares on-site).

12:00 PM

Bike Maintenance Workshop

Local mechanics walk through flat-tire fixes, brake adjustments, and a pre-ride safety checklist. Bring your own bike or use a demo unit.

1:00 PM

Advanced Group Ride — Hillside Challenge

A faster-paced 20 km route for experienced riders, with a rest stop at the halfway point.

2:00 PM

Team Relay Challenge

Form a team of four for a light-hearted relay race around the festival grounds — part obstacle course, part trivia about local bike routes. Great group-bonding activity.

3:00 PM

Scavenger Ride

Pair up and follow clues to local landmarks near the venue, snapping photos along the way. Prizes for the fastest and most creative teams.

4:00 PM

Live Music & Vendor Wind-Down

Grab food from the vendor row and enjoy a closing set from a local band while the day winds down.

After-Festival Hangout

5:30 PM · The Trailhead Café (a short ride from Scarborough Town Centre)

Keep the day going — swing by for a casual meetup with your ride group or anyone else from the festival. No sign-up needed, just show up and grab a table. A great way to trade route notes, photos, and plan the next group ride.
