Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to register in advance?

No registration is required to attend, but signing up for a specific group ride or workshop at the info booth guarantees you a spot.

Is this event suitable for kids?

Yes — the Kids' Bike Rodeo and beginner ride are designed for younger riders, and helmets are available for all ages.

What if I don't have my own bike?

Rentals are available on-site starting at $12/hr. See the Resources page for details.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — Scarborough Town Centre has accessible parking, and the festival grounds include marked accessible paths.

What happens if it rains?

The festival runs rain or shine, with group rides rescheduled or shortened only in severe weather. Updates will be posted on the community forum.

Where's the after-festival hangout?

The Trailhead Café, a short ride from Scarborough Town Centre, starting at 5:30 PM. No sign-up needed — see the Activities page for details.
