Getting There

The festival takes place at Scarborough Town Centre, 300 Borough Dr, Scarborough, ON.

On-site notes

  • Bike racks and a supervised bike valet are set up near the main entrance.
  • Group rides depart from the north parking lot every hour on the hour.
  • Wheelchair and stroller-accessible paths are marked throughout the site.
