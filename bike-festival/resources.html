Resources

Don't have a bike, or need to gear up before the festival? Here are placeholder listings for buying, renting, and prepping. Swap in real local partners for your own community.

Rent

Town Centre Bike Rentals

Hourly and full-day rentals, including kids' bikes and tandem options. Pickup right at the festival entrance.

From $12/hr · $35/day

Buy

Borough Cycle Shop

Local bike shop with new and refurbished bikes for every budget. Festival attendees get a 10% discount on same-day purchases.

Starting at $180

Gear

Helmet & Safety Kit Rental

Helmets, lights, and reflective vests available to rent on-site — required for all group rides.

$5 flat rate

Repair

Pop-Up Repair Station

Free basic tune-ups and flat repairs while you wait, run by volunteer mechanics near the main tent.

Free

Community

Bike Donation & Swap

Drop off a bike you no longer use or pick up a donated one — first come, first served.

Free / by donation

Bike Racks on Transit
